The dilemma

Everything we proved in the last twenty minutes runs straight into everything else we proved.

Students must write far more — and it has to go home
HOME
At home, AI does the writing — and the thinking

The writing they need most is the writing we can least protect.

I opened by asking how you keep AI slop out of your classroom. You said:

Two answers come up every time: check the revision history, and only let them write in class.

Those two answers were your whole defence.

Check the revision history × FAKED — you watched it happen
Only let them write in class × NOT ENOUGH — 4× won’t fit the period

Both of your instincts. Gone.

So we do the safe thing.

We pull writing back into the classroom. Timed. Supervised. Small.

To keep AI out, we quietly stopped teaching them to write.

That isn’t a solution. It’s the surrender most schools are making right now.

The whole problem, in one sentence:

How do we get students to write as much as they need — at home — without handing the thinking to a machine?

That’s what the rest of today is for.
